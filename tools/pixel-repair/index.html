Stuck Pixel vs. Dead Pixel: What Can Be Fixed?

Stuck Pixel (Fixable) A stuck pixel appears as a bright red, green, blue, or yellow dot. The subpixel transistor still has power, but the liquid crystal inside is physically stuck in one orientation. Rapid 60Hz color cycling forces the crystal to twist back and forth, often freeing it within 10 to 30 minutes.
Dead Pixel (Hardware Defect) A dead pixel is permanently black (or white on some older TN panels). The transistor is burnt out or disconnected. Software tools cannot repair dead pixels; if your screen has multiple dead pixels, consult your manufacturer warranty for panel replacement.
How to Use This Tool 1. Click "Launch Floating Exerciser".
2. Drag the small flashing square directly over the stuck dot on your screen.
3. Let it cycle for 10–20 minutes, then close the flasher to inspect the result.
