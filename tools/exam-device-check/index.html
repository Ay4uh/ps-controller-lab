Pre-Exam Checklist & Common Issues

1. Camera Permission Blocked? Click the lock/tune icon on the left of your browser address bar. Ensure Camera and Microphone permissions are set to "Allow", then reload the page.
2. Background App Conflicts Close FaceTime, Skype, Zoom, or Teams before starting your proctored exam. Another application holding an exclusive camera lock will cause a black screen.
3. Network Jitter & VPNs Most proctored test platforms (ProctorU, Honorlock, Pearson VUE) explicitly forbid VPNs. Disable corporate or proxy VPNs to avoid sudden connection drops.
